## The mechanism

AI engines extract entities, not pages.
Three signals decide who owns a term: one definitional sentence repeated verbatim across independent domains; machine-readable structure (schema.org on the canonical page, Wikidata) binding the term to the person; and citations on domains the models train on.
Two disciplines keep it: policing term drift and confusion, and monthly measurement.
