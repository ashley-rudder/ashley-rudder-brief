## The two-engine thesis (preserved from the strategy thread)

Social is the front door; the content engine is the house.
Engine one, social as the business: the visible personality, community, and front door. Real revenue, but linear: attention trades for revenue roughly one to one, capped by personal bandwidth, built on rented land, and each post dies in 48 hours.
Engine two, the content engine: one core creative act, atomized and distributed across every channel, so a single unit of work earns on eight surfaces instead of one. Social feeds it; it never replaces social.
Social is the demand signal and the top of funnel. The engine is the multiplier that turns one signal into many monetizable assets.

## The three multipliers that make it exponential

1. Repurposing: one creative act earns on eight surfaces.
2. Cross-channel discovery: every channel is both a distribution node and a discovery node for the others.
3. Catalog compounding: evergreen content and an owned list keep earning while she sleeps; the ceiling becomes the catalog, not the calendar.

## The flywheel, in order

Capture → Atomize → Distribute → Convert to owned → Monetize → Listen, and the signal picks the next Capture.
One pillar asset per week: a long recording, a build, a ranch story, a lesson.
Atomization map: vertical clips (TikTok, Reels, Shorts, for reach), full long-form (YouTube, for depth and search), written essay (blog and SEO), thread and carousel (X, LinkedIn, IG, for authority and saves), newsletter issue (email, owned, for relationship and selling), audio cut (podcast).
Rule: create once at the source; slice, never re-shoot for a channel.
Owned versus rented: IG, TikTok, YouTube, X, and LinkedIn are rented reach; the email list, SMS, community, and site are the business. Every rented interaction nudges to owned.
North-star metric early: owned audience growth and the share of revenue that depends on no single platform, never follower count.

## The phased rollout

0. Point of view: who it's for, the status quo it rejects, the promise.
1. One channel plus one weekly capture habit, repurposed to two or three surfaces, manual.
2. The owned layer: email list plus one lead magnet, all social routing to it.
3. Systematize: templates, repurposing checklist, light calendar.
4. Monetize the owned audience with the first offer.
5. Only then multiply channels; the engine exists, new surfaces are just nodes.
