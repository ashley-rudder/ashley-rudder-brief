## 2026-09-17 · Spotify launches Fresh Finds Forward · logged 9/18, corroborated

Spotify announced Fresh Finds Forward on September 17: roughly 10,000 emerging independent artists a year get 12 months of benefits.
The package: free US studio time (Electric Lady in New York, The Lighthouse at Ocean Way Nashville, Spotify Studios LA), LANDR Studio Pro and Splice Creator subscriptions, six months of BetterHelp therapy, early access to Spotify tools, tour and video partnership priority, and a Fresh Finds profile badge.
Eligibility is retroactive to January 1, 2026: an add to a Fresh Finds playlist from that date invites the artist in through Spotify for Artists.
Free to artists, and Spotify says royalty rates do not change.
Sources: Spotify newsroom 9/17 (title verified, body egress-blocked from this network), Music Business Worldwide, Music Ally 9/18, PLAYY, inmusicblog. Corroborated across independent outlets at summary level; primary bodies unreachable from this session's network, same condition the memo already discloses in its sources section.
