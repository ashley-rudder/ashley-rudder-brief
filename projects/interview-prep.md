# Interview desk: prep, feedback, implementation

Opened by Ashley's word 9/18: "we need another desk for interview prep, feedback and implementation."
Every high-stakes conversation gets a before and an after.
Interviews, pitches, panels, podcasts, client calls, mentor sessions.

## The three jobs

1. Prep. Before any named conversation: a one-page brief holding the room, the reader, the one thesis, three talk tracks, likely questions with first-sentence answers, and what winning sounds like.
2. Feedback. After any conversation with a transcript: review against the voice law in CLAUDE.md and the standing theses below. Five findings maximum, receipts quoted with timestamps, uncomfortable answer first.
3. Implementation. Feedback becomes drills and assets, never a note that dies in a doc.

## Standing theses (baseline set 9/18 from the Marketing Academy final interview review)

1. One leadership thesis per room: consolidation. Delegation and selling-the-new are its symptoms, never separate answers.
2. Point first, then one story, then stop.
3. Kill the echo-stack. One statement, then silence.
4. No courage confessions. Past fear appears only as past tense with a shipped receipt.
5. The 30-second AI proof story: one named client, what changed, one number. Owed and unbuilt.

Every future review scores these five before finding new ones.
The mouth obeys the law the keyboard already follows.

## Assets this desk owes

The AI proof story, built from raw material Ashley supplies.
The story bank: the 2009 layoff and identity, the mother and Turner Classic Movies origin, the client roster proof points, the banner line about culture becoming your consumer's favorite show.
Opening theses per room type: selection rooms, client pitches, press.

## Feed and rules

Transcripts arrive from Ashley by upload, Fireflies or Grain; the desk never fishes in her accounts beyond what she names.
Feedback in chat can be blunt; the repo copy stays clean: no family health details and no third parties' private matters while this repo is public.
Era-tag everything; a transcript is evidence of that day, never a permanent verdict on her.
Nothing sends without Ashley's word.
End of any working session: update this file, commit, push.
