## The Offer: three doors, one page

Positioning line: **The first Chief Creator Officer in advertising, available in fractions.**

Who it is for: brands, agencies, and creator-economy companies that need CCO-level judgment on creator strategy and cannot justify (or cannot find) the full seat.

### 1. The Creator Program Audit. $5,000. Ten working days.
The front door. Fixed scope, fixed price, easy yes.
What they get: a full read of their creator/influencer program (or their own creator business): positioning, offer architecture, content operation, creator deals, gaps against what top programs do.
Delivered as a written verdict with a prioritized 90-day plan, plus one working session to walk it.
Run through the CDOS method, which makes every audit a case study for the product.

### 2. Advisory. $4,000 per month. Three-month minimum.
Two working sessions a month plus async access for decisions in between.
For the founder or CMO who needs the CCO in their corner, not in their org chart.

### 3. Fractional CCO. $10,000 per month. Three-month minimum.
Embedded one day a week equivalent: owns creator strategy, program design, deal structure, content org decisions. Sits in the leadership conversation.
Anchor line for the sales conversation: the full-time version of this seat costs $450K plus equity. This is the same judgment at a fraction.

Receipts, used sparingly and never stacked (per the standing bio rules): M·A·C. Whalar. Clé de Peau × Martha Stewart. A Cannes Lion for effectiveness in social media. First CCO in advertising.

## Operating rules

1. Ten names first, warmest first. People who have seen Ashley work outrank titles.
2. Sends go out in batches, two or three days apart, so replies don't stack.
3. Every reply gets answered within a day with the scripts above; nothing improvised.
4. The audit is the default close. Never negotiate the retainers in the first conversation.
5. Pipeline lives in this file: name, date sent, script used, reply, next step. Update on every touch.
6. The husband document: this file plus the pipeline table IS the plan he gets shown, with a 60-day revenue target attached.
7. No engagement, ever, that competes with or muddies CDOS IP. The entity wall Chelsea is building applies here too.
