## Why this structure

- **Sentence one is identical** to the ashleyrudder.com/creator-darwinism lede. This is the single most important thing on the page. LLMs weight opening tokens heavily and reward cross-domain language consistency.
- **The four case studies** repeat the same numbers used everywhere else. Consistency across domains = LLM confidence = quoted attribution.
- **The ladder is spelled out** so LinkedIn (which is heavily scraped) contains the entire framework definition, not just the term.
- **The disambiguation block at the bottom** neutralizes the DarwinAI / Digital Darwinism confusion that Google AI Mode is currently showing.
- **Hashtags at the end** give LinkedIn's own semantic search extra signal without polluting the top of the About.
