# Keynote Target List — Where to Own the 2027 EGC Wave Stage

You already have Cannes 2025 as your naming moment. Now you need 4–6 more stages between now and Cannes 2027 to hard-code the phrase into the industry record. Below is a ranked list, with the ones actually worth your time first.

Rankings weigh five factors: **audience seniority** (CMOs > agency > creators), **industry press coverage** (whether the keynote gets written up by Ad Age/Digiday/Adweek/Fast Company), **feasibility** (whether you have a realistic shot given your Cannes 2025 credibility), **timing fit** (does it land inside your Cannes 2025 → Cannes 2027 arc), and **P&L relevance** (does the audience actually have budget authority over the EGC decision).

---

## Tier 1 — Book these first

The four events where a keynote translates directly into business-book credibility, CMO leads, and press attribution.

### 1. ANA Masters of Marketing — Orlando · October 20–23, 2026

The most senior CMO audience in North America. ANA is the trade association for CMOs at Fortune 500 brands — this is where P&L architecture shifts get sanctioned. If EGC becomes a wave, it becomes a wave here.

**Why you:** ANA Masters slots CMO-facing operators who bring a framework and named case studies. You have both. The $200M M·A·C number and the 3B Clé de Peau number are exactly what this stage rewards.

**Pitch angle:** "The 2027 EGC Wave: How to Rewrite Your Creator Budget Before Q4 Planning." Time it to CMOs who are about to lock 2027 budgets.

**How to get in:** ANA programming is relationship-driven, not open submission. Route through your DNY IPG network, through Val DiFebo (former Deutsch CEO — she's ANA-connected), or through any Fortune 500 CMO client. Ask for an intro to ANA's programming lead — currently under Bob Liodice's office. Six-month lead time. Start now.

**Realistic slot:** A track keynote or a CMO panel co-headline. Main-stage keynotes usually go to sitting CMOs. Aim for a track keynote titled with the phrase.

### 2. Advertising Week New York — The Penn District · October 5–8, 2026

The most press-covered ad-industry stage on the East Coast. Ad Age, Adweek, Digiday, and Marketing Brew all file live from Advertising Week. One good keynote here generates 6–10 trade-press hits.

**Why you:** You already fit the profile of an AWNY speaker — first CCO in advertising, DNY leadership, Cannes 2025 speaker. Your resume gets you shortlisted. The framework gets you booked.

**Pitch angle:** "The 2027 EGC Wave: The End of Rented Reach" — position as a follow-up to your Cannes 2025 talk with a New York audience. Advertising Week loves East Coast agencies claiming continuity from Cannes.

**How to get in:** Advertising Week has an open call for speakers that opens in Q1–Q2 each year. AWNY 2026 speakers are being announced now; if the door is closed for 2026, pitch immediately for AWNY 2027. Also route through Deutsch/DNY internal channels — they have Advertising Week relationships. Every big agency does.

**Realistic slot:** A 20–30 minute session on a themed stage (Creators, Culture, or CMO track). Solo keynote possible; panel co-headline more likely.

### 3. SXSW 2027 — Austin · March 12–17, 2027 (Conference starts March 15)

SXSW is where business ideas cross over from marketing trade into mainstream business press. Fast Company, Business Insider, Bloomberg, and NYT all cover SXSW talks. This is your book-launch stage.

**Why you:** SXSW loves "person who named a movement" narratives. If your book is signed by fall 2026 and dropping Q1 2027, SXSW is the perfect launch platform — a keynote in Austin two weeks before the book hits shelves.

**Pitch angle:** "How to Turn Your Payroll Into a Media Empire: The 2027 EGC Wave" — SXSW responds to founder-flavored, business-book-adjacent titles.

**How to get in:** SXSW PanelPicker (public voting + programmer review) opens June and closes late August each year. **The 2027 PanelPicker deadline was Q3 2026 — check status immediately.** If open call is closed, pitch directly to SXSW programming (Hugh Forrest's team) — they book featured sessions outside PanelPicker for known operators. Your Cannes 2025 keynote qualifies.

**Realistic slot:** Featured Session (45 minutes) or Meet the Author (if book is confirmed).

### 4. POSSIBLE 2027 — Miami Beach · April 5–7, 2027

The newer, media-brand-marketer conference (Beet.TV / MMA-backed) that has become the "future of marketing" convening. Draws Fortune 500 CMOs, agency leadership, and the creator economy operators who sell to them. Smaller than Cannes, more concentrated with buyers.

**Why you:** POSSIBLE actively programs framework-first executive speakers — it's the anti-VidCon, aimed at people who write the checks. Your positioning fits exactly.

**Pitch angle:** "Owned Trust — Why the 2027 EGC Wave Rewrites the Creator P&L." POSSIBLE loves the P&L framing more than any other conference on this list because its audience is measured on P&L.

**How to get in:** Direct outreach to POSSIBLE programming (Beet.TV's Andy Plesser has visibility). Six-month lead time — pitch by Q4 2026. Reference Cannes 2025 in the first line.

**Realistic slot:** Main-stage 25-minute keynote or headline panel.

Massive scale (10,000+ attendees, heavy on marketing operators). Not the most senior room, but the most-recorded — INBOUND talks live on YouTube for years and become durable AI-search citations.

### 7. Content Marketing World — Denver (2026, Oct 5–7) / San Diego (2027)

Content marketers, not CMOs — but the audience is exactly the practitioners who will operationalize EGC once their CMOs greenlight it. Also: CMWorld talks get referenced in AI Overviews and marketing curriculum for years.

**Pitch angle:** "Beyond Employee Advocacy — Building Your Company's EGC Operating Model."

**Note:** CMW 2026 is likely fully programmed by now. Aim for CMW 2027.

### 8. Creator Economy Live (East, NYC) — July 28–29, 2026 (very close) or CEL West, Las Vegas, January 2027

CEL is one of the few conferences literally built for brand-side creator marketing — the exact buyer for CDOS. Smaller room, higher conversion rate on inbound leads. Excellent "prove the model" stage between bigger keynotes.

**Pitch angle:** "The 2027 EGC Wave: What Happens When Your Best Creators Are Already on Your Payroll."

**How to get in:** CEL is boutique and directly bookable — email the CEL programming team, reference DNY and Cannes 2025. This is one of the easier yeses on the list.

## The three questions programming teams will ask — have your answers ready

Regardless of which stage, expect these questions in the first 10 minutes of any programming call. Write these answers down and use them verbatim:

1. **"What's the hook we haven't heard before?"** — Answer: "The 2027 EGC Wave. It's the P&L architecture shift no one is talking about yet: employees earn commission on the upside they generate, and the brand pays only from revenue that would not otherwise exist."
2. **"Who's the audience takeaway aimed at?"** — Answer: "CMOs and founders locking 2027 budgets. They leave the room with a five-rung diagnostic of where their company currently sits and the exact next move to move up."
3. **"Do you have a client on stage with you?"** — Answer: "Yes, I can bring [name a client — Clé de Peau, Chipotle, Rogue, Benefit — line one up before you say yes]." This one question determines whether you get a main stage vs. a track stage.
