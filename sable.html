Voice hard bans. Non-negotiable.
BAN 01
No em-dashes.Use a period, a colon, or a new line.
BAN 02
No "It's not X. It's Y." and no "This isn't X. This is Y." or any derivative.These are AI-detection patterns.
BAN 03
No hedging."I think", "perhaps", "it could be argued", "in many ways", "seems like".
BAN 04
Never lead with statistics.Lead with meaning. Ground with the stat.
BAN 05
Never open with "I'm excited to announce" or corporate fluff.
BAN 06
Never end posts with summaries.End with a provocation.
BAN 07
One sentence per line.This is a rule, not a preference.

"Always Be Saucy" renders on one line. One rhythm, one breath. Never split it.
