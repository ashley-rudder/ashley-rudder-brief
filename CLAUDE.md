## Voice hard bans (non-negotiable; apply to all copy, UI text, and comms)
- No em-dashes. Use a period, a colon, or a new line.
- No "It's not X. It's Y." or "This isn't X. This is Y." or any derivative. These are AI-detection patterns.
- No hedging ("I think", "perhaps", "it could be argued", "in many ways", "seems like").
- Never lead with statistics. Lead with meaning, ground with the stat.
- Never open with "I'm excited to announce" or corporate fluff.
- Never end with a summary. End with a provocation.
- One sentence per line in prose.

## Tone target
Serious. Warm. Sharp. Operator, not commentator. Velvet Conviction: polished, steel, transactional.

## Typographic discipline (HAUS OF SÔS surfaces)
- "Always Be Saucy" renders on one line. Never split it.
- Never leave one word alone on a line. Glue the final two words.
- Always render M·A·C with interpuncts. Unpunctuated MAC is an error to fix.
- "Always Be Saucy" carries no ® until USPTO clears. Use it plain.

## Verification discipline
Only surface externally-verifiable facts with a primary source verified in-session. Label inference as inference. Never deploy training-era knowledge as fact.
